The Climate Reality

The numbers behind why the Eco Park exists.

Every figure below is cited. Where DENSO-specific data isn't yet measured on this site, it's clearly marked "Simulation" instead of presented as fact.

1.55°C ± 0.13°C

2024: the warmest year on record

Global average surface temperature was about 1.55°C above the 1850–1900 pre-industrial baseline — the first year to be confirmed above the 1.5°C threshold.

Source: WMO, Jan 2025
local_fire_department
26 provinces > 40°C

Thailand's record 2024 heatwave

In April–May 2024, dozens of districts across Thailand's 77 provinces broke temperature records, with 26 provinces exceeding 40°C.

Source: UNICEF Thailand
flood
30,000+ families

Northern floods, same year

From August 2024, remnants of Typhoon Yagi triggered severe flooding across 12 northern provinces — alongside a drought that hit off-season rice, sugarcane and cassava elsewhere.

Sources: ThaiCyclopedia, Krungsri Research
DENSO Group's Response

Industrial precision, applied to sustainability.

DENSO Group reports a 99.96% resource recycling rate across its operations, alongside published water intake, wastewater and water-quality figures (pH, COD, BOD, SS, oil content) in its group Sustainability Report. These are group-level figures, not specific to this park site — site-level metrics are being instrumented and will replace the "Simulation" labels on the Live Dashboard as real sensor data comes online.
